All photographs in this folder are **real photographs downloaded from Pexels** and stored locally
(no hotlinked URLs). They replace the placeholder SVG silhouettes/stick figures that were generated by
`scripts/generate-images.mjs`.

- **Source:** Pexels (https://www.pexels.com)

  trademark, design mark, trade name or service mark.
- **Important caveat:** the Pexels license covers the *photo* only. It does not automatically clear
  every depicted person, artwork, logo or brand for every use. Nothing on this site should imply
  that the people shown endorse the studio or that the photos depict real studio members.
- **Names on this site:** dancer/instructor names (Aarohi, Kavin, Isha, Rithik, Diya, Arjun,
  Meera, Sana) are website profile labels. The photographs below are **illustrative stock
  representations**, not authentic portraits of the named people, unless authentic portraits are
  supplied later.
- **File handling:** downloaded from the Pexels CDN (`images.pexels.com`) with
  `auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=…` at display-appropriate widths (850–1400 px). Real file
  extensions are preserved (all selected photos are JPEG). Original placeholder SVGs were left in
  place.
